# Scope of Work: Northgate Depot EV Charging Hub

**Client:** Northgate Transit Authority (NTA)
**Document:** NTA-SOW-2026-014 Rev B
**Location:** Northgate Bus Depot, Unit 4 Riverside Industrial Estate

## 1. Background
NTA is replacing 40 diesel buses with battery-electric buses from Q4 2027. The depot needs
overnight charging for the full fleet plus two rapid chargers for opportunity charging.

## 2. Scope
2.1 The Contractor shall design, supply, install, test and commission:
  a) 20 x 150 kW dual-outlet DC depot chargers (40 bays) with pantograph-ready mounting;
  b) 2 x 350 kW rapid DC chargers;
  c) a new 11 kV / 0.4 kV substation (2 x 2.5 MVA transformers) and LV switchboards;
  d) cable containment, ducting and civils for all bays, including bay marking;
  e) a charge management system (CMS) integrated with NTA's fleet management system via OCPP 2.0.1;
  f) 1 MWh battery energy storage system (BESS) for peak shaving.
2.2 The Contractor shall manage the DNO connection application and coordinate the DNO's
    contestable and non-contestable works.
2.3 The depot remains operational throughout. Works in live bus lanes are limited to 22:00-05:00.

## 3. Programme
3.1 Contract award is planned for 2 February 2027.
3.2 Phase 1 (20 bays) shall be operational by 30 September 2027.
3.3 Full completion shall be achieved by 15 December 2027.

## 4. Requirements
4.1 All equipment shall comply with IEC 61851 and IEC 62196.
4.2 The CMS shall achieve 99.5% availability measured monthly.
4.3 The Contractor shall hold ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 certification.
4.4 Design shall comply with BS 7671 (18th Edition) and the IET Code of Practice for EV Charging.
4.5 The Contractor shall provide 5 years of maintenance with 4-hour response for critical faults.
4.6 Cyber security of the CMS shall meet IEC 62443-3-3 SL2.
4.7 Training shall be provided for 30 depot staff.

## 5. Commercial
5.1 Contract form: NEC4 ECC Option A (priced contract with activity schedule).
5.2 Delay damages: 15,000 per day, capped at 10% of contract value.
5.3 Retention: 5%, half released at completion, half after a 24-month defects period.
5.4 Performance bond: 10% of contract value.
5.5 Payment: monthly, 30 days from valid invoice.
5.6 Liability cap: 100% of contract value; unlimited for data breach.

## 6. Not included
Bus procurement, fleet telematics and depot building alterations are excluded.
