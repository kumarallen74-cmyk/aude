# Driver surveys and NPS (v1.47.0)

**Operate → Surveys.**

## Settings

- **Ask drivers after a charge:** on. The driver app shows the question on the receipt of a finished charge, to drivers
  signed in with their phone number.
- **Question:** empty for the standard one ("How likely are you to recommend us to a friend?", in the app's language);
  a custom question is shown as written.
- **Share of charges asked:** 1–100 %. The same charge always gets the same answer, so a driver who comes back to the
  receipt is not asked about a different charge.
- **At most once every N days** per driver (default 14).
- **Low scores open a support case:** a score of 6 or less with a comment becomes a case in Operate → Support, linked
  to the session (when the support desk is on).

## Reading it

NPS is the share of promoters (9–10) minus the share of detractors (0–6), from −100 to +100. The page shows it overall,
by week, per site and per charger (lowest first), the tags drivers chose (speed, price, availability, payment, the
app, location, cleanliness, something broken; red when mostly from detractors), the distribution of scores and each
response with its comment and case.
